Steps to Verify OTP & Access Dashboard

1. OTP Verification

  • If you are already registered, please enter your registered email ID and click the Send OTP button. Wait a few seconds to receive a One-Time Password (OTP).
  • Check your email inbox for the OTP, enter it within one minute, and click the Verify OTP button.
  • Once verified, you will be redirected to your personal dashboard.

2. Dashboard Overview

  • Your dashboard displays your personal and academic details.
  • Locate and click the Select button next to your name or student ID.
  • You will see three options: Topic Update, Advance Payment, and Full Payment.

3. Topic Update

  • If you haven’t selected a topic yet, you can manually add one here.
  • If you’ve already submitted a topic and wish to change it, you must do so before making the advance payment. Topic changes will not be allowed after the payment process begins.

4. Payments

  • Advance Payment – for partial upfront payment.
  • Full Payment – to complete the entire payment at once.
  • Clicking either option will take you directly to the available payment methods.

Please follow the above steps carefully to complete your project registration process.
